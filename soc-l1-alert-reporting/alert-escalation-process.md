# SOC Alert Escalation Process

In a Security Operations Center, alerts that cannot be resolved by a Level 1 analyst must be escalated to higher-tier analysts.

---

## Escalation Workflow

1. Alert detected in SIEM
2. Level 1 analyst performs initial investigation
3. Evidence is documented
4. Alert is escalated to Level 2 analyst if suspicious activity is confirmed

---

## Reasons for Escalation

- Potential compromise
- Advanced attacker techniques
- Need for deeper forensic analysis

---

## Example Escalation

In this lab investigation, alerts related to:

- Phishing email activity
- Possible web shell compromise

were escalated to the **Level 2 SOC Analyst**.

---

## Importance of Escalation

Proper escalation ensures that serious incidents receive deeper investigation and rapid response.
